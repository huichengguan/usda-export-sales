US Grain Export Inspections & Physical Vessel Flow Analysis

Publication Schedule & Source: Export inspections are officially certified under the U.S. Grain Standards Act by the USDA Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS) & Federal Grain Inspection Service (FGIS) and published every Monday at 11:00 AM US Eastern via report WA-GR101 (and the open data platform agtransport.usda.gov), measuring physical metric tons inspected as ships and railcars load at U.S. export ports.

📄 Official USDA WA-GR101 Physical Realization vs Contract Sales
🚢 Monday Inspections (What You See Here):

Measures actual physical grain weighed & loaded into ocean vessel holds or cross-border railcars during the week. This is hard, physical confirmation that commodities are physically leaving the United States.

📋 Thursday Export Sales (FAS ESRQS):

Measures commercial forward sales contracts signed between exporters and overseas buyers. Includes future delivery commitments and remains subject to cancellations, rollovers, or destination changes.
